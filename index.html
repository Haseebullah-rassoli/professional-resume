Suggested Phrases & Vocabulary

Pulled directly from Chapters 5 and 6. Click "Copy" and paste straight into the form above — then edit the specifics so every line honestly reflects your own experience.

Professional Summary Formula

[Job Title] with [X] years of experience in [industry/specialty], specializing in [2–3 key skills]. [One standout, quantified achievement].

Example:
Product Manager with 6 years of experience in B2B SaaS, specializing in roadmap strategy and cross-functional delivery. Led the launch of a platform redesign that grew active users by 22% within two quarters.

Strong Action Verbs (by category)

Leadership: Led, Directed, Managed, Coordinated, Mentored
Achievement: Delivered, Achieved, Exceeded, Launched, Won
Improvement: Streamlined, Automated, Optimized, Reduced, Increased
Analysis: Analyzed, Identified, Evaluated, Forecasted, Diagnosed
Creation: Built, Designed, Developed, Established, Implemented

Bullet-Point Formula

[Action Verb] + [specific task or scope] + [measurable result]

Example:
Led a cross-functional team of 6 through a platform migration, cutting deployment time by 35% in 4 months.

Avoid:
Responsible for managing platform migrations for the team.

Words & Phrases to Avoid

Avoid: "Responsible for...", "Duties included...", "Helped with...",
"Assisted in...", "Various tasks", "Team player" (with no evidence),
"Hardworking", "Detail-oriented" (as a bare claim, unsupported)

Replace with a specific action verb + a measurable result instead.
